# Draft: Right-to-Know request to the Philadelphia Parking Authority

Status: DRAFT, not sent. Send it with the PPA's current request form (see "Before you send").

---

**To:** Right-to-Know Officer, Philadelphia Parking Authority, 701 Market Street, Suite 5400, Philadelphia, PA 19106
**From:** [your name, mailing address, email, phone]
**Date:** [date]

Under the Pennsylvania Right-to-Know Law (65 P.S. § 67.101 et seq.), I request copies of the following records in electronic, machine-readable format (CSV preferred):

1. A record of every on-street parking citation issued in the City of Philadelphia from **January 1, 2018 to the most recent date available**, with these fields for each citation, matching the fields previously published on OpenDataPhilly ("Parking Violations"):
   - anonymized ticket number
   - issue date and time
   - violation description
   - fine amount
   - issuing agency
   - street address / location of the violation
   - latitude and longitude, where recorded
   - zip code
2. Records showing whether the PPA continued to supply parking-violation data to the City of Philadelphia for publication on OpenDataPhilly after December 31, 2017, and any decision or policy to stop doing so.

I am not requesting license plate numbers or any other personal information about vehicle owners. Please redact or omit the plate and state fields (or provide the anonymized plate ID used in the earlier public release) as needed.

I request that fees be waived because the records will be used for a public-interest civic data project. If fees exceed $[amount], please contact me before processing.

If any part of this request is denied, please cite the specific exemption and release the remainder.

Thank you,
[name]

## Before you send

- Officer: Alanna D. Buchanan, Esq. (per that page, as of when I checked). Her email is hidden on the page; get it from https://philapark.org/contact/ or the RTK Policy PDF linked on the transparency page. An older policy PDF lists OpenRecordsOfficer@philapark.org, but I could not confirm it is current.
- Fee schedule and response deadline were not shown on the page; check the linked PDFs. (Pennsylvania's RTKL generally gives agencies 5 business days to respond, extendable by 30 days. Verify this against the PPA policy.)
- The City's own open-records page says City requests do not cover the PPA, so this has to go to the PPA directly.
