Ticket hotspot forecast

Ranks ~200 m zones of Philadelphia by expected parking tickets for a chosen day, from 2012–2017 history. Same inputs always give the same answer.

How accurate is it?

Backtest on held-out 2017 days: the top 50 zones (under 1% of the city) captured about 29% of that day's tickets, close to the 34% best possible in hindsight; picking 50 zones at random captures about 1%. Weekday-by-weekday differences are small on weekdays and larger on weekends. Details: full backtest results.

Limits: no holiday, weather, event or enforcement-staffing inputs; data ends in 2017 so forecasts for later dates assume the 2012–2017 pattern still holds; 4-hour time windows; zones are ranked by where tickets were issued, which reflects past enforcement as well as parking demand.
